Markdown Blockquote Rendering Specification
==========================================

Scope
-----
Define how mdview renders Markdown blockquotes so quoted text keeps visual
cues, nested levels remain distinct, and interior emphasis markers stay
predictable. Cover fixture layout, styling expectations, nesting behavior,
and tolerance for inline emphasis inside quotes.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering prefers ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` when available and falls back to
  a plain-text formatter otherwise.
- Source line order and spacing must remain unchanged. Renderers may add quote
  prefix cues but must not reorder or collapse text.
- Tests rely on small fixtures under ``resources/tests`` and assert on visible
  cues rather than brittle snapshots.

Rendering Rules
---------------
- Recognize a blockquote when a line starts with ``>`` followed by a space or
  other text. Preserve any content following the marker on the same line.
- Apply a consistent quote prefix (e.g., leading ``>`` with a space or a Rich
  style equivalent) for each quoted line. In fallback mode, surface the literal
  ``>`` markers so quoting remains visible.
- Preserve interior spacing and punctuation. Do not trim leading or trailing
  spaces inside the quote content.
- Keep blockquotes scoped to contiguous quoted lines. Do not merge separate
  quote blocks across blank lines or unquoted text.
- Allow inline emphasis (italic, bold, combined) inside quotes. Styling must
  remain contained without leaking into the quote prefix or neighboring text.

Nesting Expectations
--------------------
- Support nested blockquotes by stacking markers (``>>`` or ``> >``). Each
  nesting level must keep its own prefix cue so depth remains visible.
- Prevent level merging: a single ``>`` line must not adopt styling from a
  deeper ``>>`` line, and vice versa.
- Maintain alignment so nested prefixes do not consume content characters. The
  quote content should align relative to its own prefix width.

Fixture Inventory
-----------------
- ``resources/tests/markdown/blockquote_basic.md``: A single-level blockquote
  with surrounding prose. Asserts that quote prefixes appear and spacing
  remains intact in Rich and fallback modes.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/blockquote_nested.md``: Two-level quote stack with
  parent and child lines. Confirms each level keeps distinct prefix cues and
  does not merge indentation or styling.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/blockquote_emphasis.md``: Quotes containing
  ``*italic*`` and ``**bold**`` markers. Verifies emphasis styling stays inside
  the quote content and does not alter prefix cues.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/blockquote_spacing.md``: Quotes with leading and
  trailing spaces plus punctuation near the markers. Checks that whitespace and
  punctuation persist unchanged.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/blockquote_multiline.md``: A multi-line quote
  separated from surrounding text by blank lines. Ensures quotes stay grouped
  without merging into adjacent paragraphs.

Exit Criteria
-------------
- All fixtures above exist under ``resources/tests/markdown`` and align with
  the rendering and nesting rules.
- Renderers show visible quote prefixes at every depth, preserving spacing and
  punctuation in both Rich and fallback modes.
- Inline emphasis within quotes renders as emphasis without affecting quote
  prefixes or adjacent text outside the blockquote.
