Markdown Horizontal Rule Rendering Specification
===============================================

Scope
-----
Define how mdview renders Markdown horizontal rules so ``---`` and ``***``
markers produce predictable separators, avoid stray trailing text, and stay
consistent across rich and fallback renderers.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering prefers ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` when available and falls back
  to a plain-text formatter otherwise.
- Source spacing and line order must remain unchanged; renderers must not
  reorder surrounding paragraphs or trim blank lines that separate content
  from rules.
- Tests draw fixtures from ``resources/tests`` and assert on visible cues
  rather than brittle snapshots.

Separator Recognition
---------------------
- Recognize a horizontal rule when a line contains at least three hyphens
  (``---``) or at least three asterisks (``***``) optionally separated by
  single spaces. Ignore leading and trailing spaces around the markers.
- Treat mixed markers (e.g., ``-*-``) or lines with fewer than three eligible
  markers as plain text, not as horizontal rules.
- Do not accept additional non-space characters on the line. If text follows
  the markers, render the entire line as literal text to avoid leaking
  trailing content past the separator.

Rendering Rules
---------------
- Render a recognized rule as a separator cue distinct from surrounding
  paragraphs (e.g., a solid line or styled divider). In fallback mode, surface
  a plain line of hyphens matching the canonical ``---`` marker count.
- Preserve surrounding blank lines. Insert at least one newline before and
  after the rendered separator so adjacent paragraphs remain separate.
- Suppress any trailing text: when a rule is recognized, no additional text
  should appear on the separator line in output.
- Keep rules isolated to their lines. Do not merge with neighboring content or
  extend styling beyond the separator line.

Fallback Behavior
-----------------
- When rich rendering is unavailable, emit a user-facing warning indicating
  the fallback plain-text separator is active for horizontal rules.
- In fallback mode, display the literal marker line (normalized to ``---``)
  and preserve surrounding spacing without additional styling.

Fixture Inventory and Formats
-----------------------------
- ``resources/tests/markdown/horizontal_rule_basic.md``: Contains standalone
  ``---`` and ``***`` lines with surrounding paragraphs. Asserts that both
  markers render as separators with preserved spacing.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/horizontal_rule_spacing.md``: Includes rules
  with optional interior spaces (``- - -`` and ``* * *``) and leading or
  trailing spaces. Confirms recognition tolerates spacing while rejecting
  mixed markers.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/horizontal_rule_text_suppression.md``: Provides
  lines where markers are followed by text or digits. Validates that such
  lines render as literal text and that recognized rules never leak trailing
  content.
