Markdown Nested List Rendering Specification
===========================================

Scope
-----
Define how mdview renders nested Markdown lists so parent-child indentation
remains visible, levels do not merge, and separation stays consistent across
renderers. Cover both bulleted and ordered parents with mixed child markers
while preserving existing single-level list rules.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering prefers ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` and falls back to a plain-text
  formatter when Rich is unavailable.
- Input spacing is authoritative; renderers preserve author-indented levels
  without collapsing interior whitespace.
- Fixtures live under ``resources/tests/markdown/lists`` and avoid unrelated
  Markdown constructs such as blockquotes or fenced code blocks.
- Assertions focus on list cues—indentation depth, prefixes, and separation—
  rather than brittle full-output snapshots.

Rendering Rules
---------------
- Recognize nested items when a bullet or numeric marker appears after at
  least two leading spaces or a single tab relative to the parent marker.
- Maintain consistent indentation per depth so child markers align vertically
  within the same level and visibly offset from parents.
- Preserve marker characters (``-``, ``*``, or ``1.`` style numbering) at
  every level. In fallback mode, surface literal prefixes without styling
  loss.
- Allow inline emphasis and links inside items while preventing styling from
  leaking into markers or between levels.
- Keep each item on its own rendered line; do not concatenate parent and child
  text even when children immediately follow parent lines in the source.

Indentation and Separation Expectations
---------------------------------------
- Preserve blank lines between surrounding prose and the first or last list
  item so nested lists remain visually distinct.
- Maintain a single blank line between parent and child blocks when present in
  the source; otherwise, render contiguous items without inserting extra
  spacing.
- Prevent merging adjacent levels: a child block must not inherit the parent's
  marker or collapse back to the parent depth when siblings follow.
- Support heterogeneous markers: a bulleted parent may contain ordered
  children and vice versa without reformatting either marker style.

Safeguards Against Level Merging
--------------------------------
- Treat reduced indentation as a deliberate return to a higher level rather
  than an error. Preserve the new depth and its marker even if spacing varies
  slightly between items.
- Reject zero-indent child candidates: items without indentation relative to
  the current level remain siblings of their nearest matching-depth item.
- Do not collapse nested items when a parent line wraps; continuation lines
  must align with the item's own text, not with child indentation.

Fixture Inventory
-----------------
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/nested_bulleted.md``: Bulleted parents with
  bulleted children at varying depths. Confirms aligned indentation, preserved
  markers, and separation from surrounding prose.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/nested_mixed_markers.md``: Bulleted parents
  with ordered children and ordered parents with bulleted children. Verifies
  heterogeneous marker retention without reformatting.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/nested_spacing.md``: Nested lists with
  intentional blank lines between depth changes. Ensures separation is
  preserved and parent-child merging does not occur.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/nested_wrapping.md``: Parents and children
  whose text wraps across lines. Validates that wrapped text does not reset
  indentation or collapse levels.

Exit Criteria
-------------
- All fixtures above exist under ``resources/tests/markdown/lists`` with
  content that reflects indentation, separation, and anti-merging rules.
- Renderers display clear indentation per depth with visible markers in Rich
  and fallback modes.
- Parent and child levels remain distinct even with mixed markers, wrapped
  text, or spacing variations.
