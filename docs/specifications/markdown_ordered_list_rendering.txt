Markdown Ordered List Rendering Specification
============================================

Scope
-----
Define how mdview renders Markdown ordered lists so numbering stays visible,
continuations remain aligned, and spacing is predictable. This specification
covers single-level ordered lists. Nested list behavior is handled by a
separate document.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering prefers ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` and falls back to a plain-text
  formatter when Rich is unavailable.
- Inputs may start numbering at values other than ``1`` and may repeat or
  reset numbering across disjoint list blocks.
- Fixtures live under ``resources/tests/markdown/lists`` and avoid mixing
  ordered lists with nested structures or bullet markers.
- Assertions focus on numbering cues, alignment, and separation rather than
  brittle full-output snapshots.

Rendering Rules
---------------
- Recognize list items that start with an integer followed by ``. ``. Preserve
  the digits exactly as written, including leading zeros, without normalizing
  or renumbering.
- Render item prefixes with consistent indentation so digits and separators
  align vertically within the same list. In fallback mode, surface the literal
  ``1. `` style prefixes without trimming interior spacing.
- Keep each list item on its own rendered line. Do not merge item text with
  adjacent items or surrounding paragraphs.
- Allow inline emphasis inside list items while preventing styling from
  leaking into numbering prefixes or neighboring lines.

Numbering Continuation and Wrapping
-----------------------------------
- Preserve source numbering even when a list resumes after intervening text;
  do not restart at ``1`` unless the source does.
- Maintain indentation for wrapped lines within a single item so multi-line
  content aligns under the text column, not under the numeric marker.
- Accept varied digit widths (for example ``1.`` versus ``12.``) and ensure
  subsequent item text columns align despite differing prefix lengths.

Spacing and Separation Expectations
-----------------------------------
- Maintain a blank line between surrounding prose and the first or last list
  item so lists remain visually distinct.
- Preserve single blank lines between ordered items when present in the
  source; treat double blanks as intentional breaks rather than collapsing
  them.
- Avoid merging adjacent ordered lists that restart numbering after a blank
  line; treat the restart as a distinct block even when numbering overlaps.

Fixture Inventory
-----------------
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/ordered_incrementing.md``: Sequential list
  starting at ``1`` with leading and trailing prose. Verifies alignment,
  separation from prose, and literal prefixes in fallback mode.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/ordered_offset_start.md``: List beginning at
  ``3`` to confirm non-default starts render the authored digits without
  renumbering and remain aligned with subsequent items.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/ordered_wrapped_items.md``: Items whose text
  wraps onto additional lines. Ensures wrapped lines indent beneath the text
  column rather than beneath numeric markers.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/ordered_restart.md``: Two ordered lists
  separated by prose where numbering restarts at ``1``. Confirms lists remain
  distinct and numbering does not merge across blocks.
- ``resources/tests/markdown/lists/ordered_wide_numbers.md``: Items with
  double-digit prefixes to validate column alignment when digit width
  increases within the same list.

Exit Criteria
-------------
- All fixtures above exist under ``resources/tests/markdown/lists`` with
  content that reflects numbering, alignment, and spacing rules.
- Renderers display aligned numeric markers, preserve authored numbering
  across list restarts, and maintain wrapped-line indentation under the text
  column in both Rich and fallback modes.
- Ordered lists remain visually separated from surrounding prose and from
  adjacent ordered lists that intentionally restart numbering.
