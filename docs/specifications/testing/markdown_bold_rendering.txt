Markdown Bold Rendering Specification
====================================

Purpose and Scope
-----------------
Define fixtures and expectations for rendering Markdown bold text in
isolation. Keep coverage focused on bold emphasis markers and their
interaction boundaries without coupling to headings, lists, or links.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering uses ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` when available and falls back to
  plain text otherwise.
- Fixtures live under ``resources/tests/markdown/bold`` and avoid mixing bold
  markers with unrelated Markdown constructs unless explicitly stated.
- Assertions target bold styling cues (e.g., ANSI intensity or clear text
  delimiters) instead of brittle full-output snapshots.
- Output targets a monospace terminal that preserves ANSI styling sequences
  when Rich is active.

Fixtures
--------
- ``bold_asterisks.md``: paired ``**`` markers around single and multiple
  words, separated by surrounding plain text to confirm boundaries are tight.
- ``bold_underscores.md``: paired ``__`` markers covering single words and
  phrases to verify underscore emphasis mirrors asterisk handling.
- ``bold_adjacent_text.md``: bold spans immediately followed and preceded by
  plain text without spaces to ensure markers do not swallow neighbors.
- ``bold_nested_with_italic.md``: nested italic inside bold (``**_text_**``)
  and adjacent italic after bold to confirm style containment and order.
- ``bold_with_punctuation.md``: bold spans containing commas, periods, and
  trailing punctuation to ensure punctuation remains inside the emphasis span.
- ``bold_unclosed_marker.md``: a lone opening ``**`` and ``__`` to confirm
  renderers leave unmatched markers literal instead of applying styling.

Rendering Expectations
----------------------
- Bold spans render with clear intensity cues under Rich. The fallback
  renderer preserves the literal text and markers so readers can infer
  emphasis.
- Bold styling starts immediately after the opening marker and ends before the
  closing marker without trimming interior spaces or punctuation.
- Adjacent plain text retains its spacing. No characters are duplicated,
  reordered, or dropped at bold boundaries.
- Nested italic inside bold renders both styles when Rich is active. When
  falling back, markers remain literal but correctly scoped. Styling never
  leaks past the closing bold marker.
- Consecutive bold spans separated only by whitespace remain distinct and do
  not merge into a single emphasis block.

Failure and Edge Cases
----------------------
- Unmatched opening or closing markers render literally with no bold styling
  applied, and they must not corrupt following text.
- Mixed marker types (opening ``**`` with closing ``__``) are treated as
  literal text rather than partially styled spans.
- Inputs with mixed line endings (``\n`` and ``\r\n``) produce consistent
  rendering cues without losing markers.
- Rendering must remain stable under Python 3.7 environments and avoid new
  dependencies beyond the existing project stack.

Test Coverage Outline
---------------------
- Validate each fixture renders the expected bold cues and preserves literal
  markers in fallback mode.
- Assert that nested italic remains contained and that adjacent plain text is
  untouched by bold parsing.
- Confirm unmatched or mixed markers stay literal and do not trigger styling
  or swallowing of neighbor characters.
