Markdown Heading Rendering Specification
=======================================

Purpose and Scope
-----------------
Define fixtures and expectations for rendering Markdown headings in isolation.
Keep coverage limited to heading syntax (``#`` through ``######``) without
combining with other Markdown elements. Apply these rules to terminal output
produced by the default renderer and its plain-text fallback.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering uses ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` when available and falls back to
  plain text otherwise.
- Fixtures live under ``resources/tests/markdown/headings`` and avoid mixing
  headings with unrelated formatting.
- Assertions focus on structural and styling cues rather than full snapshot
  comparisons.
- Output targets a monospace terminal that preserves ANSI styling sequences
  when Rich is active.

Fixtures
--------
- ``h1_and_h2.md``: level-1 and level-2 headings with surrounding blank lines
  to verify separation and hierarchy cues.
- ``h3_through_h6.md``: consecutive headings from level 3 through level 6 to
  confirm distinct prefixes and non-collapsing ordering.
- ``heading_with_inline_punctuation.md``: a single heading containing commas,
  periods, and a trailing colon to ensure punctuation remains verbatim.
- ``heading_adjacent_text.md``: a heading followed immediately by body text on
  the next line to confirm the renderer does not swallow or merge content.
- ``heading_with_inline_formatting.md``: a heading that includes emphasis
  markers inside the heading text to ensure inner styling does not escape the
  heading boundary.

Rendering Expectations
----------------------
- All headings emit a trailing newline after the heading block so that
  following text begins on a new line.
- Level 1 and level 2 headings render with stronger visual cues (e.g., bold or
  underline tokens) than deeper levels. When Rich is unavailable, prefixes and
  spacing still signal hierarchy.
- Levels 3 through 6 retain their ``#``-derived hierarchy cues without losing
  characters or collapsing into one another.
- Inline punctuation and spacing inside headings render exactly as provided
  and do not strip colons, commas, or periods.
- Inline emphasis markers inside headings render as styled text when Rich is
  active and remain literal when the fallback renderer is in use. Styling does
  not leak into subsequent lines.
- Adjacent body text begins after the heading's terminating newline. No
  heading consumes the first character of the following paragraph.

Compatibility and Edge Cases
----------------------------
- Inputs with mixed line endings (``\n`` and ``\r\n``) must parse consistently
  and produce the same rendered output cues.
- Leading or trailing spaces before the ``#`` prefix are treated as literal
  text rather than heading markers to match standard Markdown behavior.
- Empty headings (``#\n``) render as blank lines without emitting stray style
  markers.
- Rendering must remain stable under Python 3.7 environments and avoid
  dependencies beyond the existing project stack.

Test Coverage Outline
---------------------
- Validate that each fixture produces the expected heading hierarchy cues and
  trailing newlines without snapshot brittleness.
- Assert that inline formatting stays contained within the heading span and
  does not alter surrounding content.
- Confirm fallback behavior still surfaces heading structure through prefixes
  or spacing when styling is unavailable.
