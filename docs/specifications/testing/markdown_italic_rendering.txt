Markdown Italic Rendering Specification
======================================

Purpose and Scope
-----------------
Define fixtures and expectations for rendering Markdown italic text in
isolation. Keep coverage focused on italic emphasis markers and their boundary
rules so styling does not leak into neighboring content or reorder text.

Assumptions
-----------
- Rendering uses ``rich.markdown.Markdown`` when available and falls back to
  literal text otherwise.
- Fixtures live under ``resources/tests/markdown/italic`` and avoid mixing
  italics with unrelated Markdown constructs unless explicitly stated.
- Assertions target italic styling cues (ANSI italics or clear delimiters)
  instead of brittle full-output snapshots.
- Output targets a monospace terminal that preserves ANSI styling sequences
  when Rich is active.

Fixtures
--------
- ``italic_asterisks.md``: paired ``*`` markers around single words and
  phrases with surrounding plain text to verify tight boundaries.
- ``italic_underscores.md``: paired ``_`` markers covering single words and
  phrases to confirm underscore emphasis matches asterisk handling.
- ``italic_adjacent_text.md``: italic spans touching plain text without
  interior spaces to ensure markers do not consume neighbor characters.
- ``italic_with_punctuation.md``: italic spans that include commas, periods,
  and trailing punctuation so punctuation stays inside the emphasis span.
- ``italic_nested_with_bold.md``: bold inside italic (``*_text_*``) and
  italic followed by bold to confirm style containment and ordering.
- ``italic_unclosed_marker.md``: lone opening ``*`` and ``_`` to confirm
  renderers leave unmatched markers literal instead of applying styling.

Rendering Expectations
----------------------
- Italic spans render with clear slant cues under Rich. The fallback renderer
  preserves literal markers so readers can infer intent when styling is
  unavailable.
- Styling begins immediately after an opening marker and ends before the
  closing marker without trimming interior spaces or punctuation.
- Adjacent plain text keeps its spacing, and no characters are duplicated,
  dropped, or reordered at italic boundaries.
- Nested bold inside italic renders both styles when Rich is active. When
  falling back, markers remain literal but correctly scoped. Styling never
  spills past the closing italic marker.
- Consecutive italic spans separated only by whitespace stay distinct and do
  not merge into a single emphasis block.

Failure and Edge Cases
----------------------
- Unmatched opening or closing markers render literally with no italic
  styling applied, and they must not corrupt following text.
- Mixed marker types (opening ``*`` with closing ``_``) render as literal text
  rather than partially styled spans.
- Inputs with mixed line endings (``\n`` and ``\r\n``) produce consistent
  rendering cues without losing markers.
- Rendering must remain stable under Python 3.7 environments and avoid new
  dependencies beyond the existing project stack.

Test Coverage Outline
---------------------
- Validate each fixture renders the expected italic cues and preserves literal
  markers in fallback mode.
- Assert that nested bold remains contained and that adjacent plain text is
  untouched by italic parsing.
- Confirm unmatched or mixed markers stay literal and do not trigger styling
  or swallowing of neighbor characters.
